# Encore

Charts, habits, Wrapped and playlist tools for your Spotify listening, in one page.

- **Charts**: top artists, songs and albums for any week, month, year or all time; listening clock, heatmaps and streaks; how your taste changed; skips and loyalty.
- **Artists**: your history with each artist, deep cuts and similar artists.
- **Playlists**: smart playlist generator, cleanup, sort, merge and split.
- **Discover**: forgotten favorites, deep cuts and new artists (via Last.fm).
- **Wrapped** for any year or month, **milestones**, and shareable image cards.

Everything runs in your browser. Your listening history and Spotify login are stored only on your device.

## Setup

1. Open the site and go to **Settings**.
2. Create a Spotify developer app at [developer.spotify.com/dashboard](https://developer.spotify.com/dashboard), add the redirect address shown in Settings, tick **Web API**, and paste the Client ID.
3. Request your **Extended streaming history** at [spotify.com/account/privacy](https://www.spotify.com/account/privacy/) and drop the .zip into Settings when it arrives.
4. Optional: add a free [Last.fm API key](https://www.last.fm/api/account/create) for similar-artist suggestions.
